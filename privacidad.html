3. Meal photos and technique videos

We do not store your photos or your videos. Ever.

When you photograph a meal, the image is sent to our AI provider, analyzed, and the reply comes back. We save only the result (calories, macros, food names). The image itself is not written to any database or file storage of ours.

When you record a 10-second technique video, the video never leaves your phone. Your own browser extracts 6 still frames, shrinks them to 768 pixels and sends only those frames for analysis. Neither the video nor the frames are stored by us.

Please avoid photographing other people, documents, screens or anything you would not want analyzed. If a face appears incidentally in a frame, it is still not stored by us, and our AI is instructed not to guess age, sex, identity or any personal attribute from it.

7. How we protect it

  • Everything travels encrypted over HTTPS, with HSTS enabled.
  • Your database row is protected by row-level security: your account can read and write only your own row, enforced by the database itself, not just by our code.
  • We never store card numbers. Stripe handles payment data.
  • The site sets a strict Content Security Policy, blocks framing, and locks camera, microphone and geolocation permissions off by default.
  • Pro features are verified on the server against Stripe, so a leaked receipt link cannot unlock somebody else's subscription.

No system is perfect. If we ever discover a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities as required by California law and by any other law that applies to you.
